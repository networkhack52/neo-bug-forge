## Posting tips (keep these in mind)
- **Hook in line 1** — LinkedIn truncates after ~2 lines; earn the "see more".
- **Reply to every comment** — half the algorithm, all of the relationship.
- **Product ~1 in 3 posts** — mostly value/POV, occasionally the pitch.
- **Best times:** weekday mornings (your audience: US + EU security/GRC).
- **End with a question ~half the time** to drive comments.
