Questions

Straight answers.

What is Attestly?
Attestly is a self-serve tool that answers security questionnaires — SIG, CAIQ, and custom vendor questionnaires — for B2B SaaS companies. It drafts each answer from your own SOC 2 report and security policies, cites the exact source, and reuses your approved answers so every questionnaire gets faster.
Does it hallucinate or make up answers?
No. Every answer is grounded in your uploaded documents and approved library, and each one shows the exact source it came from. When your documents don’t support a confident answer, Attestly flags it for review instead of inventing a certification or control.
Do I have to talk to sales or sign an annual contract?
No. Attestly is fully self-serve — sign up, upload a questionnaire, and get answers back. There’s a free tier (25 answers) and paid plans start at $99/month. No demo required.
What questionnaire formats does it support?
SIG, SIG Lite, CAIQ v4, VSAQ, and custom vendor questionnaires in .xlsx or .csv — including multi-column templates. Answers are written back into your original file so you return the customer’s own template, filled in.
How is it different from Vanta, Loopio, or Conveyor?
Those are enterprise platforms — sales-led, expensive, and bundling questionnaires into a larger suite. Attestly is built for small and mid-size SaaS teams: self-serve, affordable, and focused on one thing — answering questionnaires with provable, cited answers.
Is my data secure?
Your documents are used only to answer your own questionnaires — never to train models. Credentials and API keys are hashed, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you review every answer before anything is sent.
How fast is it?
A full questionnaire is answered in minutes, not days. Reused answers are instant; the rest are drafted in parallel.
